We are a team of five: three programmers (Waldron Wang, Junyoung Oh and Arjun Prinjha), an artist (Jaden Nhan) and a composer (Regis Gurung). We spent the first two days talking through the concept together and drafting most of our game design document. From there we split the work by strengths. Waldron handled coordination: he set up the GitHub repository and the Unity project, built a shared code skeleton, turned the design document into GitHub issues and scheduled our meetings. Everyone then took ownership of their own part of the game, and our regular meetings kept us in sync and gave us a place to solve problems together.

Our design grew from one idea: in Recolor the Heart, the player brings seven colors back to a gray city. Each color has its own district and boss, and beating that boss restores the color and gives the player a new ability. We organized the project so each color lives in its own scene with one owner, which let us all build in parallel without overwriting each other's work. The programmers divided up the bosses, levels, abilities and interface, Jaden gave the world its look, and Regis composed a theme for each district. After each round of work we played the newest build together and everyone gave feedback on everyone's work. That feedback shaped most of our changes: bosses were made harder or easier, the Blue level was rebuilt as a vertical climb, and the interface was cleaned up after playtests, until each color's look, sound and fight felt like one piece.
