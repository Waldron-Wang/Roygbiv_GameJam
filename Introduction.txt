Recolor the Heart is a 2D action platformer about feelings. Once, every color had a place in the heart, until the Violet Sovereign decreed “no more feeling” and drained the world to gray. Starting from one spark of yellow, you travel through seven color districts. Each district is built around an emotion, like warmth, anger, envy or sadness, and ends in a boss fight. Every boss you beat brings its color back to the world and gives you a new ability, such as a dash, a double jump or slowing down time. That ability helps you reach the final showdown with the Sovereign and make the heart whole again.
